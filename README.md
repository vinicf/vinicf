### 👨‍🏫 About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in Information Systems and a researcher in Computer Networks and Telecommunications. I holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the Institute of Computing at **Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF)**, Brazil.

My research spans **wireless communications, network softwarization, Internet of Things (IoT), and non-invasive e-Health sensing**, aiming to build resilient, next-generation network architectures for smart environments and healthcare.

* 🎓 **Ph.D. in Computer Science** — Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF)

### 🔬 Research Interests

- **Wireless Networks & HetNets**: Multi-technology connectivity, network coverage planning, and optimization.
- **5G/6G & Network Softwarization**: Network slicing, infrastructure sharing, roaming, and V2X communication frameworks.
- **Internet of Things (IoT) & WBANs**: Wireless Body Area Networks, channel-aware transmission protocols, and realistic simulation modeling.
- **e-Health & Non-Invasive Sensing**: Remote patient monitoring using Wi-Fi Channel State Information (CSI) and vital sign analytics.
- **Smart Cities & Urban Mobility**: Intelligent transport systems, location-aware services, and mobile connectivity.
